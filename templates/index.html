Algorithm Concept Note

Main Idea: Explores the graph level by level from the source using a FIFO queue, so every city one road away is checked before any city two roads away.

Node Selection: Always expands the shallowest unexpanded city (the one that has been waiting in the queue longest).

Information Used: Depth (number of road segments) only. Ignores road miles and any estimate of distance to the goal.
